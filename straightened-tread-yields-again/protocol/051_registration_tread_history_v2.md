Question: after one overload, can a tread-shaped metal strip be pressed visually near straight while the same downward loading initiates **new plastic flow** below its virgin first-yield load? The comparison is idealized, not a procedure for repairing or rating an installed stair. "Earlier renewed plastic flow" is the strongest permitted interpretation; do not say material yield stress, elastic stiffness, collapse strength, fatigue life or safe load decreased. Do not call residual-stress-induced re-yield the Bauschinger effect: primary material has no Bauschinger law.

## Fixed physical experiment

One ideal homogeneous, initially straight rectangular aluminium strip: horizontal captive pin/roller span L=0.800 m, width b=0.280 m, thickness t=0.012 m, E=70.0 GPa, initial f_y=110 MPa, density 2700 kg/m³, g=9.81 m/s². Both supports resist up *and* down vertical forces, permit end rotation; the roller releases axial motion. They are a laboratory fixture, not an ordinary loose stair bearing. Plane sections, symmetric through-thickness bending, small displacement, no initial manufacture stress. At every stage carry self-weight w=ρbtg. A vertical patch load of total signed P is uniform along the central a=0.100 m and the full width; positive P means downward. The same specimen/fixture/patch and positive diagnostic are used in every branch.

Analytic virgin first-yield reference: I=bt³/12; M_y=f_y I/(t/2); centre patch moment coefficient c=L/4−a/8; gravity moment M_g=wL²/8; `P_y=(M_y−M_g)/c`. For each q, fresh virgin gravity equilibrium → P increases to exactly 1.25 P_y → unload to P=0 → reverse P decreases to −Q where `Q=qP_y` → unload to P=0 → diagnostic P increases to the common endpoint **1.50 P_y**. Freeze all 51 q values `0,0.025,...,1.250`. The q=0 branch is the identical bent-but-not-straightened control. Only Q varies. Any shared cached prefix must be byte/numerically identical to 51 independent fresh specimens.

At each zero-patch, gravity-loaded state, define `B(q)=max_{x∈[0,L]} |v_q(x)−v_virgin,gravity(x)|`, evaluating extrema in the **continuous interpolated span**, not just its midpoint or station values. Select q_star **only by minimum B** on the frozen grid, before inspecting reload R. Cubic Hermite interpolation uses computed station deflections and slopes; solve derivative roots inside every interval and include ends. If minimum B intervals overlap under the numerical envelope, all overlapping cells are the co-minimum set; each must pass the R release gate. An arbitrary other qualifying cell cannot rescue q_star. `R(q)=P_re(q)/P_y`, where P_re is the first *additional* accumulated plastic strain on positive diagnostic reloading at any fibre, including the **outer surface** of a previously yielded zone.

## Retained constitutive state and event

At each x and signed thickness coordinate z retain signed plastic strain εp, nonnegative accumulated plastic strain p, isotropic radius R_iso=H_iso p, kinematic backstress α. For trial total strain ε=ε0+κz, set `σ_trial=E(ε−εp_old)`, `η=σ_trial−α_old`, `f=|η|−(f_y+R_iso_old)`, `Δγ=max(0,f)/(E+H_iso+H_kin)`. Only after κ and ε0 converge for the external moment/zero axial force, commit `σ=σ_trial−EΔγ signη`, `εp=εp_old+Δγ signη`, `p=p_old+Δγ`, `α=α_old+H_kinΔγ signη`, `R_iso=H_iso p`. Perfect plasticity: H_iso=H_kin=0. For the symmetric baseline, ε0=0 follows from the antisymmetric state, but independently integrate both signs of z and require N≈0; if symmetry fails, solve ε0 instead of forcing it. Never commit trial/iteration states or reset history on unload/reverse. A demonstrably elastic unload may use exact Δκ=ΔM/EI; otherwise solve incrementally and retain the same state law.

At P=0 after the history, **total** stress must integrate to the gravity moment M_g(x), not zero. The *history-excess* stress `σ_q−σ_virgin,gravity` integrates to zero extra axial force and moment. For first renewed yield, freeze the post-history state and increase P elastically: the trial stress at each fibre follows its exact elastic moment increment, with outward yield function derivative `df/dP>0`. Find the smallest positive P where f crosses zero outward; a merely tangent contact within `1e−8 f_y` is not an event. Confirm with independent nonlinear trials just below and above (bracket ≤0.005 P_y). The diagnostic endpoint 1.50 P_y must lie above every event. The exact q=0 control is `P_re=1.25 P_y` (same history reloaded to prior maximum), required within 0.005 P_y. Independently calculate at least one reverse-history **stress-block** case from piecewise analytic integration, not by reinvoking the production return map.

## Numerical plan, uncertainty and gates

Use composite Simpson integration through the full thickness, including both surfaces: 101 equally spaced signed z nodes in production, 201 in refinement. At least 81 uniformly spaced longitudinal stations plus the **exact** central-patch edges; refine to 161 plus edges. Use 100 equal increments on initial overload, at least four substeps per 0.025-q interval on reverse loading (≥200 total), and 100 on each unload unless the exact elastic-unload check passes every fibre throughout. Refine increments to 200/400 **with spatial and fibre grids fixed**. Use final elastic trial stress at the surfaces for event detection, not an interior Gauss point. Relative moment equilibrium residual ≤1e−7 of M_y, axial force residual ≤1e−7 of f_ybt, reaction closure ≤0.1% of total applied load with an absolute 0.1 N floor.

Independently validate (a) virgin P_y and linear M=EIκ; (b) virgin ideal-plastic rectangular pure-bending identity `M/M_y=1.5−0.5(κ_y/κ)²` at κ/κ_y=1.2,1.5,2.0 within 0.5%; (c) q=0 exact re-yield; (d) a separately integrated signed reverse stress-block benchmark; (e) gravity-off sign-reversal symmetry; (f) elastic unload slope EI; (g) statically determined section moments/reactions and zero history-excess resultants. These anchors check different things; numerical self-consistency alone is not independent validation. At every load stage require maximum |total fibre strain|≤0.005, maximum |slope|≤0.12 rad, maximum |deflection|/L≤0.04; otherwise the small-deflection beam interpretation is killed.

At q=0, each candidate bow minimum and its two neighbors, vary longitudinal stations **alone**, fibre nodes **alone**, load steps **alone**, and reload-event bracket **alone**. Extend refinement to every additional q whose B uncertainty overlaps the minimum; do not assume only one. For each observable, form a conservative operational uncertainty as the **sum of absolute shifts** from these four changes (no cancellation or quadrature optimism), plus half the event bracket for P_re. The denominator P_y also carries the max analytic-vs-numeric verification gap; ratio bounds use adverse numerator/denominator combinations. Require `u_B≤0.005 mm` (absolute, not 0.1 mm) and `u_R≤0.005` at any released minimum. No division by a 0.1 mm bow "floor": B0 is expected near 0.67 mm, so such a floor could swallow the entire near-straight criterion. If B is effectively zero, report its **absolute** envelope, not a percentage convergence. Require q minimum stable within one 0.025 grid interval (or report a co-minimum range).

The **primary perfect-plastic release** requires, at every q in the bow co-minimum set, `upper[B(q)]/lower[B(0)]≤0.10` and `upper[P_re(q)]/lower[P_y]≤0.90`, with P_re>0 and scope/validation gates passing. The 90%-bow-removal and 10%-earlier-flow cutoffs are manager-set *editorial materiality thresholds chosen after the disclosed exploratory point-load screen*; they were not fixed before any exploration, but are frozen now before the registered patch/gravity run. They create a clearly separable result beyond the ≤0.5%-ish numerical envelope, not an engineering standard or human visual-acuity measurement. If the nearest-straight q fails either, do not switch to an arbitrary other q or relax the cutoffs.

Sensitivity uses the **same q_star (or entire co-minimum set) selected by perfect-plastic B alone**, never a reoptimized q. Run isotropic H_iso/E=0.01, H_kin=0 and kinematic H_kin/E=0.01, H_iso=0 as separate uncalibrated constitutive assumptions. Run a one-at-a-time physical perturbation matrix `a=0.090/0.110 m` and `f_y=99/121 MPa`, recomputing that case's virgin P_y and applying the same dimensionless overload/q_star. For each of these six legs require `upper[B(q_star)]/lower[B(0)]≤0.20` **and** `upper[P_re(q_star)]/lower[P_y]<1.00`, plus all scope/verification gates. A failure kills the broad viewer headline; the ideal-only finding may remain in the report but **no 051 video is released** under this protocol. H/E=0.01 is a sensitivity assumption, not proof of applicability to a particular aluminium alloy. State every sensitivity result; do not delete or retune a failing leg.

Prototype timing budget before sweep: ≤60 s per q-equivalent case. Aggregate **≤30 min** for the 51-cell primary sweep and **≤30 min** for all independent validation/refinement/sensitivities, hard ceiling 60 min compute. If exceeded, profile shared-prefix/section-vectorization without changing numerical conditions; if it cannot fit, stop and redesign. Do not consume 255 min under a per-cell 5-min excuse.
